# Pulse

Style: luminous geometric arcade with crisp silhouettes, a subtle plum gradient, and a quiet star field. Dark rounded panels frame high-contrast, sentence-case utility text.

Palette: void #12001B, panel #20152F, line #49345E, mint/player #54F1AA, violet/hazard #BB77FF, gold/score #FFC461, blue/shield #53C7FF, pink/impact #FF6A96, ink #F3EDF9, muted #BEB0CD.

Gold is a solid diamond. Hazards have four sharp points. Shields have a circular outline and a cross. The fixed mint signal and its curved wake track the direction of travel. Three separated blue segments around the signal show banked shields once the first shield is collected. During Pulse, the outer rim holds steady and a light-blue arc drains and refills within it. Keep glow subordinate to the bright silhouettes and use the same shapes in the title vignette, legend, and playfield.

Distant stars drift on each navigation screen. A hit breaks the hazard into filled and outlined bubbles that spread and fall as the field carries them behind the signal. The fatal splash uses mint and violet and plays in slow motion through the open center of the results screen. Floating numerals drift with the field and grow within a limited range as rewards increase. Soft chimes distinguish gold and shields. Breakthrough, shield loss, and death use progressively deeper impact sounds. Reduce Motion keeps an impact fade and holds the starfield and post-run scenery still.
